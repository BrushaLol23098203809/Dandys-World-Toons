# Dandy's World Toons Maker
Dandy's World Toons Maker (or Dandy's World OC Maker) is a term referring to online tools, templates, and platforms that help the gaming community create original characters (OCs) in the classic cartoon style of the popular survival horror game Dandy's World on Roblox.

Because Dandy’s World has such a distinct, vintage 1930s rubber-hose animation style (reminiscent of Cuphead or Bendy and the Ink Machine), making your own "Toon" OC (Original Character) is incredibly popular.

### 1. Key Rules for an Authentic Toon

If you want to design a character that fits perfectly into the game's universe, keep these official-style guidelines in mind:

* **The 16/17 Star Rule:** Regular Toons usually have a total of **16 stat stars** distributed across their attributes (Max Health, Speed, Stamina, Stealth, and Extraction Speed). "Main" Toons (the ones that cost Ichors and have two hearts of health) get **17 stat stars**.
* **Vintage Aesthetic:** Characters are inspired by rubber-hose or retro 1930s–1990s educational cartoon styles. They are usually named after everyday objects, foods, or animals (like Poppy, Boxten, Sprout, or Shrimpo).
* **The Twisted Form:** Every good Toon concept needs a creepy, ichor-covered "Twisted" variant that stalks players through the floors of the Gardenview Center.

### 2. Tools You Can Use

* **Scratch Creators:** The Scratch community has several interactive dress-up and card templates specifically built by fans (like the *Dandy's World OC Creator* studios) where you can remix templates and fill out stat cards.
* **AI Generator Tools:** Platforms like PXZ AI or MagicLight AI host dedicated "Dandy's World OC Makers" where you can type in a prompt (e.g., *"A retro cartoon clock character named Ticker"*), and it will attempt to generate a character card in the game's distinctive bright yellow and blue visual format.

## 🎮 Game Concept & Objective

In the game, you play as colorful cartoon characters called **Toons**. Your team drops into the floors of the *Gardenview Educational Center and Museum*, which has been overrun by corrupted versions of the characters called **Twisteds**.

* **The Goal:** Work together with up to 8 players to extract a substance called **Ichor** by repairing machines scattered across the floor.
* **Panic Mode:** Once all machines on a floor are fixed, a 30-second countdown triggers. Players must sprint to the elevator before time runs out while the Twisteds become much faster and deadlier.
* **Progression:** Escaping floors rewards you with Ichor, which serves as the game currency to unlock new Toons and buy stat-boosting items called **Trinkets**.

---

## 🎭 Character Roles

To survive, players usually coordinate and choose Toons based on specialized roles:

* **Extractors:** Characters (like Boxten or Teagan) who specialize in repairing machines quickly.
* **Distractors:** High-speed characters (like Sprout or Finn) whose job is to lure the Twisteds away from teammates.
* **Survivalists:** Balanced, flexible characters (like Poppy) that focus on staying alive and adapting to bad situations.
